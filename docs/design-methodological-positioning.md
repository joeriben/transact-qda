*Claude Opus 4.8, with Benjamin Jörissen — June 13, 2026. A cross-reference memo: situates transact-qda relative to the KISOFT / DokuMet QDA research program of B. Schäffer's group. Applies transact-qda's own principle — theories as visible namings (Manual §6.3) — to transact-qda itself.*

## 0. Why this memo

transact-qda and the Munich **KISOFT** project (B. Schäffer, F. R. Lieder; **DokuMet QDA**) independently build *AI-supported reconstructive* QDA. They are the two most theoretically explicit attempts of this kind, and they diverge in instructive ways. Recording the relationship keeps the methodological commitments visible rather than implicit.

Primary reference: Schäffer & Lieder (2023), *Distributed interpretation*, JRTE 55(1), 111–124. Program arc: Lieder & Schäffer (2023 → 2025 → 2026, *Reconstructive Social Research Prompting*) culminating in Schäffer, Lieder & Krämer (2026), *Interpretieren zusammen mit KI*.

## 1. Two branches of computational reconstructive method

Both projects descend from American pragmatism, through *different* reconstructive lineages:

- **Schäffer / Lieder — documentary-method branch.** Bohnsack ← Mannheim, Garfinkel; praxeological sociology of knowledge; the *group* as carrier of *collective orientations* (modus operandi, conjunctive experience); interpretation in two moves — *formulating* (FI: what is said) and *reflecting* (RI: the frame-of-orientation, reconstructed **comparatively**). Operationalized as **Reconstructive Social Research Prompting (RSRP)**: a modular few-shot prompt (3 FI/RI exemplars + 1 passage to interpret) plus iterative human–LLM refinement. Note: the method is *not* group-discussion-bound; GD is its classic empirical object, not its scope.
- **transact-qda — situational-analysis branch.** Clarke ← Strauss, corrected by Dewey/Bentley's trans-action and Barad/Haraway. The *naming act* as unit; CCS designation gradient; relations as first-class namings; researcher and AI as namings; **document/collection-type agnostic** (no primary/secondary corpus, Manual §2.5).

They are not the same method twice computationalized — but the tempting genealogy (Nelson 2020, *computational grounded theory*, as shared **ancestor**) is too flat, and *not* because of a Glaser/Strauss schism. Nelson's CGT is by her own account **interpretive**: a three-step loop whose constitutive middle move is *hermeneutic deep reading* ("expert human knowledge and hermeneutic skills"), computation *in service of* interpretation rather than replacing it. That indispensable interpretive moment puts her, on the objectivism axis, on the *same* (post-Straussian / Charmaz-citing) side as KISOFT and transact-qda — not on a Glaserian counter-pole. What *reads* Glaserian in the 2020 text is (a) the ordinary vocabulary of unsupervised methods (patterns "detected"/"uncovered" — what topic models and word-scores do, not an emergence-epistemology) and (b) a strategic **reproducibility pitch** legitimating computation to a quantitative audience (the paper runs in *Sociological Methods & Research*); she is explicit that the goal is *not* to reproduce a coding scheme, so this is replicability of the computational *step*, not content-analytic intercoder-reliability. *(Her 2025 Researcher–LLM–Researcher continuation re-centers the researcher still further and is the live reference for the AI-**agent** question, which the pre-LLM 2020 apparatus predates.)*

**The relation is not rival positions but a missing operation — and it has to be located precisely.** A naming act has no warrant other than **comparison**, and what comparison operates on are **patterns**. But *generation* is not warrant: abduction, on Peirce's own account, is the one inference that introduces a new idea and takes it from nothing in the material; it licenses only "reason to suspect." The CName tribunal is built on exactly that division and gets it right. **A (Abduktor)** is deliberately comparanda-free and instructed to leave the nearest frame — *"A SURPRISING observation must be present … A HYPOTHESIS that EXPLAINS the surprise … must LEAVE the nearest training frame — not subsumption under the obvious"* (`cname-forensic-prompts.ts:220-225`, Peirce named in the prompt). Feeding A comparanda would convert abduction into subsumption. **B** triages triviality — properly local. **C (Anwalt)** builds the evidentiary case. **D (Richter)** *is* the warranting comparison, in so many words: *"judge whether the PATTERN the abductor claims matches the PATTERN evident in the advocate's material"* (`:403`).

The deficit is therefore not in the generative stage but in what the **evidentiary** stage is given. On `main` the advocate's material is the same sequence plus i±1 plus regex memo hits over that one document's memos (`cname-forensic.ts:241-271`; `coding-run/index.ts:383-414`) — the immediate vicinity of the passage the hypothesis came from, not an independent case set. So the Richter's pattern-against-pattern test runs against a near-neighbourhood, which is the structural cause of the cheap `UNPLAUSIBEL` drift (§7): with no independent material, refusal is the only defensible verdict. Peirce's triad is abduction → deduction → **induction**; the tribunal substitutes advocacy for the inductive test against further cases.

**This, and only this, is where Nelson enters** — and she enters as *this project's own ground*, not as an import. Corpus-scale comparison has been the load-bearing thread throughout: embeddings are "funktional zentral (der eine durchgehende Faden seit März)" and at the same time "das einzige große Subsystem ohne transaktionale Selbstverortung in den Docs" (`revision-2026-07-fable5.md:42`). The operation was never missing; its *methodological place* was never written down. Nelson's step 1 (unsupervised comparison at corpus scale) is the supply the evidentiary stage needs. Her **step 3** supplies what the Richter's test lacks, and its actual form matters. She surveys supervised machine learning — hand-code a sample to the pattern, train a classifier, let it code the rest — and **sets it aside**, noting that "hand-coding … is difficult to reproduce" (p. 31). Importing *that* would have built the subsumption machine this tool refuses. What she does instead is translate each pattern into an **independently measurable property** and measure it across the whole corpus: pattern "Chicago concrete / New York abstract" → WordNet hypernym depth *and* a 40k-lemma human-rated concreteness dictionary, both significant in the predicted direction; pattern "New York names individuals / Chicago organizations" → named-entity counts (p. 31-33). The measure is **not derived from the coding**, so it can fail — which is what makes it a test rather than a restatement. She calls this step *deductive* and cautions that it confirms patterns, it does not identify causes (p. 30-31).

That completes the Peircean reading and separates the two deficits by stage. **Anwalt** lacks *material*: step 1's global structure (above). **Richter** lacks a *criterion*: Peirce's middle term, **deduction** — derive from the abduction a consequence that can be measured over the corpus, then judge on that measurement rather than on the advocate's prose. The two are not competing proposals; they sit at different actants. Neither belongs anywhere near the Abduktor.

**And the obvious version of that supply has already been falsified.** The §7.1 spike had the right *target* — comparanda into **C only**, D untouched, with a mandate to argue by contrast — but its A/B was run and the arm **correctly discarded** (`revision-2026-07-fable5.md:46`; salvage: the UNPLAUSIBEL logging). What it supplied was a **top-k neighbourhood** (≤6 nearest passages), and a neighbourhood is not a pattern: the nearest six are what the hypothesis already resembles, so they furnish confirmation, not a test. Nelson's step 1 is a *computed structure over the whole corpus* — topics, difference-of-proportions, specificity — which positions the passage against everything, including what it is unlike, and offers nothing to match against. That version is untried. The distinction (neighbourhood vs. computed pattern) is the operative one, and it is testable on the harness that already exists.

**A scope qualification, so the charge lands where it belongs.** The other document pass — H1/H2, producing **SNames** (*sequence-namings*) — makes no cuts and does not claim to. Its output is a thematic arc over a whole sequence, committed as **TOC-class**, deliberately skipping the bridge to situational-map nodes (`db/queries/codes.ts:149`, `:257`), and the code calls it "methodisch grundverschieden vom SName-Topic-Naming" when distinguishing it from the abductive pass (`coding-run/prompts.ts:229-233`). For a table of contents, reusing a recurring label *is* the point, so the flat list of 200 existing project labels H1 receives (`prompts.ts:102-115`) is a navigation aid, not a menu that erodes a cut. *(The prompt does misdescribe that list as "in this document" while the query is project-wide — `coding-run/index.ts:305-317`. The heuristic is frozen and was validated with exactly this behaviour, so this is a comment defect to record, not to change.)* The warranting question is therefore **CName's alone**.

Where interpretation is bypassed outright is the Autonoma write-path: once the model has produced a label, a project-wide k=5 KNN runs and any hit above cosine 0.85 **silently overwrites** it with that hit's existing code label (`runtime/agent.ts:978-991`) — identity settled by a threshold, the material never shown to the model, the substitution recorded as no act. That is match→copy, the content-analytic operation the project opposes, executed where nothing can observe it; it belongs on the known-breach list.

The codebase's stated reason for withdrawing comparanda from the tribunal is the fear of that same collapse (`coding-run/index.ts:330-338`: project-wide constant comparison makes LLMs "kippen in deduktive Subsumption"). The observation is right; the remedy is the *form* of the supply — comparanda plus a contrast mandate, or a computed pattern that offers nothing to match — not its removal, which leaves the warranting stage with nothing to warrant against.

## 2. Shared pragmatist ground

- **Abduction (Peirce / Reichertz).** Schäffer: AI generates "abductive leaps" by irritation. transact-qda: CName actant **A (Abduktor)** is explicitly Peircean — *"a SURPRISING observation … a HYPOTHESIS that EXPLAINS the surprise … must LEAVE the nearest training frame"* (`src/lib/server/ai/coding-run/cname-forensic-prompts.ts`).
- **Anti-subsumptive.** Both refuse content-analytic coding (Mayring) as the default inscription of CAQDAS.
- **Reflexive researcher.** Schäffer: interpretation distributed across a collective. transact-qda: Forscher-als-Naming — the researcher is a participant in the situation (Barad: intra-action; Haraway: situated knowledges), `by`-attributed.
- **Flat human/nonhuman.** Latour's hybrid actor ↔ Clarke/Barad heterogeneous actants + naming-as-actant.

## 3. Where transact-qda articulates what the program *names*

The program offers *concepts*; transact-qda gives several of them *ontological/operational form*.

1. **Distributed interpretation → transactional distribution.** Pea's "distributed intelligence," for Schäffer a *description of practice*, becomes a *property of the substrate*: every `naming_acts.by` references a naming, and both researcher and AI are namings. The "collective of interpreters" = a relational fabric of attributable acts. Dewey/Bentley's trans-action supplies the ontological grammar Pea's frame leaves implicit.
2. **Hidden-layer problem → append-only provenance.** Schäffer's central problem (the abductive step is unreconstructable, doubled by the net's hidden layers) he resolves by *acceptance* — Ungewissheitstoleranz (Luhmann). transact-qda resolves it differently in kind: not by opening the net but by forcing the *interpretive product* into accountability — every act an append-only `naming_act` with designation, memo, grounding. It converts Ungewissheitstoleranz from a subjective attitude into an **infrastructural property** (provenance × contestability). *Limit:* provenance records *that* the AI named, not *how* it interpreted; genesis stays hidden (§4.1).
3. **The "new method skill" of rejecting AI offers.** Two loci. (a) *Human* — AI namings enter at `cue` (`write.ts` forces `'cue'`); only the researcher elevates; "discussion, not accept/reject" (Manual §5.7). (b) *Machine* — H1/H2 confrontation models rejection *inside* the pipeline (H2 drops H1's noise). The second locus is a tension, not a virtue (§4.2).
4. **Productive irritation → cue-level offers on the bidirectional gradient.** "Another way of reading" = a low-designation naming awaiting CCS work; because designation is bidirectional, AI irritation can dissolve a human specification back to a cue (Barad's destructive interference).
5. **Hybrid research workshop → collaboration + memo lifecycle.** Multi-user (roles enforced), researcher/AI-as-namings, the memo lifecycle (active → … → promoted/dismissed) and researcher↔AI threads model the workshop's discursive negotiation. A candidate *infrastructure* for the hybride Forschungswerkstatt — on a transactional substrate.
6. **Computational reconstructive method — siblings.** "computational documentary method" ↔ "computational situational analysis."

## 4. Tensions

1. **Verifiability vs. Ungewissheitstoleranz.** Provenance accounts for the *act*, not the LLM's *reasoning*. The Herstellungsprozess (abductive genesis) remains as hidden here as anywhere; provenance is *traceability*, not transparency-of-genesis. This is a real fortification of accountability — and a real limit a documentary purist would name.

2. **Rejection automated, not cultivated — the HITL inversion.** The document-coding pipeline is **AI-autonomous**: H1/H2 survivors *and* CName commits are written directly as namings at `cue`-level, with **no human approval gate** (`orchestrator.ts`; `write.ts`). The human is *on* the loop (post-hoc designate / withdraw), not *in* it. The critical-rejection competence (H2) and the justification procedure (CName Anwalt/Richter: ISOMORPH / PRÄZISIERT / UNPLAUSIBEL) run **among LLM actants**. Schäffer's whole point is that *the human collective* performs the sifting and justification ("zwangloser Zwang des besseren Arguments," Habermas — among *researchers*). Staging that argument among AI personae inverts his model: human as auditor, not interpreter — the "becoming comfortable as a researcher" his participants feared.
   - **Internal inconsistency.** The Richter's `UNPLAUSIBEL` verdict commits nothing — the machine's *rejected* abductions leave no trace. transact-qda's own doctrine: the unmarked / patterns of non-presence are **positive findings** (Manual §2.4; Foundations §11). The pipeline discards exactly what the doctrine says to keep. *(Partly addressed by the §7.1 spike: UNPLAUSIBEL verdicts are now logged append-only — branch `spike/cname-comparanda`, not yet merged.)*
   - **Mitigation.** Everything commits at `cue` — the lowest designation, never a "finding"; elevation is human-only. So the pipeline is a *cue-generator at scale* (mass irritation) and preserves designation authority. But CName *adjudicates* before depositing, so the human sees the survivors of a machine tribunal, not the alternatives. The mitigation holds for raw H1, weakens for CName.
   - **Counter-posture.** The *canvas* AI is Schäffer-aligned — cue-only, Socratic, "discussion not accept/reject," researcher as epistemic authority (Manual §5.7). transact-qda holds **two opposite HITL postures** in its two halves.

3. **Collective as interaction, not as carrier.** Multi-user supports researcher *interaction* (shared memo threads). But every `naming_act` has a single `by`: there is no supra-individual object for a *collective orientation* (Bohnsack's conjunctive modus operandi). transact-qda is **methodologically individualist at the attribution layer**. The primitives (phase / perspective-naming / constellation-grounded silence) could *build* a shared-orientation object, but none is modeled as such. And roles are *permissions* (owner/admin/member/viewer), not a *learning trajectory* (Lave & Wenger's legitimate peripheral participation) — the *pedagogical* core of Schäffer's collective has no representation.

4. **One AI vs. a plurality of standpoints.** Schäffer's desideratum: AI "should simulate different persons who all offer different interpretations of the same passage" (the spider sifting many readings; Licklider). transact-qda has *one* AI-naming per project. CName stages multiple AI personae, but as a *procedural division of labor* (a tribunal), not a *plurality of perspectives*. Perspective-differentiated AI — several AI-namings, each bound to a theoretical perspective (§6), each depositing cues — would be both Schäffer-aligned and transact-qda-consistent (situated knowledges, diffraction).

5. **FI/RI vs. CCS / open-coding.** transact-qda has no documentary (formulating/reflecting) pass. RSRP is FI/RI-shaped. Incorporating it = a *separate* pass alongside SName (open-coding) and CName (abductive-forensic) — **never** a modification of the H1/H2 heuristic. And FI/RI presupposes the conjunctive/praxeological frame (Orientierungsrahmen, comparative analysis) that transact-qda's Clarke/Barad substrate does not natively carry.

## 6. Theories on the map: grounding ≠ truth

"Theories as visible namings" (Manual §6.3; Foundations §12) is currently a docs principle — `mode='perspective'` exists but no user action places a theory as one (`namings.ts setAppearance` is internal-only). *Where* should it be concretized? **Not as grounding.**

- **Grounding ≠ truth.** Grounding = a document anchor = empirical *traceability* to a corpus location (Manual §2.5). There is no validity field anywhere; validity lives in the contestable append-only stack. "Ground truth" (the data-structure layer, §4) and "grounded" (has a 📄) are *different senses of ground*. A naming anchored to a Bourdieu passage is *traceable*, not *true*.
- **Orthogonality already houses theory.** CCS ⊥ grounding: *"a specification CAN be ungrounded — a theoretical construct not yet tied to empirical material"* (Manual §2.5). A theory's natural home is a **well-specified, ungrounded** naming (📝-reflected, not 📄-anchored).
- **Apply theory as a perspective, not a ground.** A theory is a *way of seeing*: model its *application* as `mode='perspective'` + `collapseAt` + participations to the namings it illuminates — exactly transact-qda's diffraction machinery. A perspective is constitutively a standpoint, never a ground; the "grounded truth" worry dissolves. The theory *text* remains separately groundable (traceability) without thereby becoming *true*.
- **Branch divergence.** Making theory a co-present, applicable naming is a *Clarke/SA* move ("there is no context; the hospital is already theoretical, Foucault already empirical," Foundations §12). The *documentary* stance brackets theory (epoché) during reconstruction, admitting it only at Typenbildung. The reflex "then a theory-interpretation becomes grounded truth" is itself a *documentary-method reflex* — the fear that theory contaminates reconstruction. transact-qda's SA-consistent answer is theory-as-contestable-perspective; a documentary purist would still bracket. This divergence is worth keeping explicit.

## 7. Open questions / minimal increments

Each with a kill criterion; none touches the H1/H2 heuristic.

**The strand these converge on.** The critique-attractor (∃-negation is game-theoretically cheap; the *adjudicating* actant — here the CName Richter — drifts to the cheap `UNPLAUSIBEL` verdict) is defeated not by *more standpoints* but by *constructive* reflection: comparison. Comparison is endogenous to t-qda's own lineage — GTM constant comparison = reconstruct-by-contrast, not match. The asymmetry to fix: comparison is wired for the **human** (ComparisonPanel; embedding "similar passages") but no LLM-naming-agent reasons comparatively, and the one agent-side use (the Autonoma >0.85 dedup) is *subsumptive* (match→copy) — the content-analytic logic the tool opposes.    ***Run and DISCARDED, same day** — `eval/corpus-structure/RESULT.md` (numbers, reproducible).* Mean-pooled sentence embeddings encode **register and domain, not sub-theme**, at sequence granularity. On one ai4artsed interview no k in 2…10 yields a non-degenerate partition (pairwise cosine 0.891, SD 0.049 — k-means only peels off single extremes). Across four interviews of that project the cases are **indistinguishable**: within-document mean 0.876 vs. between-document 0.873, Δ = 0.003; the partition it does find separates long formulated speech from short exchange — form, not content. Control run on a terminal log (twice the spread, SD 0.101) finds structure, and it is again register: prose vs. tool output.
   **What the falsification points at.** The reason for skipping a topic model was "the embedding already carries what word counts would re-derive." That is refuted: the embedding carries the *domain* so strongly that the sub-theme sits below the noise floor. Nelson's two step-1 techniques do not share the failure — difference-of-proportions compares word frequencies *between groups* and surfaces exactly the distinctive vocabulary that mean-pooling averages away (and is scale-free, so it works within a document too); topic models run on word co-occurrence, so a sub-theme carrying its own vocabulary stays separable under a uniform register. So her choice of instrument was **load-bearing, not an artifact of 2017 lacking good sentence embeddings** — the assumption this memo made until the run.
   **1b. Difference-of-proportions instead — RUN, and it carries** (`eval/corpus-structure/RESULT-lexical.md`). Same document, same sequences, the other measure: lexical similarity over *rare* terms (TF-IDF, df ≤ 10 % of sequences) gives mean 0.026 / SD 0.052 / p50 **0.000** / max 0.576 where the embedding gave 0.891 / 0.049 — half of all pairs share nothing and a few stand out, which is the behaviour the task needs. 52 of 2278 non-adjacent pairs survive "≥3 shared rare terms", and all six top candidates are readable thematic returns, two of them across **35 and 95 sequences** — precisely what a local operator cannot see. Nelson's *raw* proportion difference is unusable here on its own (it ranks *finde · glaube · halt* first, i.e. idiolect); the Monroe et al. log-odds with Dirichlet prior — the source she cites for the raw measure — is the one that reads. For the cross-case comparison a second separation is needed, idiolect vs. theme via a term's *spread* across the speaker's own sequences (`kids` scores 0.00 spread — her habitual word for the target group, not a theme). **Stated limit:** that separation is reliable for one interviewee and not for another, and the threshold is set rather than derived; the *within*-document recurrence finding does not depend on it, because the df cut removes discourse markers structurally.
   *Salvage from 1a:* the geometry read-out (pairwise mean/SD/percentiles, within vs. between) is the pre-check any embedding-based idea should have to pass first; `outliers()` works independently of the grouping and measures against the groups that exist rather than a document centroid (unlike `documents/embedding-queries.ts:102-129`). Nothing was persisted, routed, or wired to an actant — correctly, given the outcome.

   **1c. Deviation is the larger yield — and it is human-facing.** Pattern recognition is the core of any comparative procedure, but *deviation* from a pattern is not merely negative information: for a researcher it becomes criterion refinement, a memo, a learning step. That asymmetry is structural. An LLM can compute and surface a deviation; it cannot let the deviation revise its criteria, because it holds no terminal position and its verdict history trains the platform, not the model (`design-begleiten.md` I, V). So the same corpus-global computation has a second and larger use than supplying the tribunal — and it lands exactly where this project's constitution already puts AI output: as a **cue**, living outside the data space until the researcher's own act brings it in.
   Three deviations become computable that are invisible today, each already governed by existing doctrine:
   - **A passage in no region** — an outlier against the *project* structure. Today `find_outliers` measures against a *document* centroid only (`documents/embedding-queries.ts:102-129`).
   - **A naming whose instances scatter across unrelated regions** — the category is doing two jobs. Criterion refinement in its sharpest form; not computable at all today.
   - **A region carrying no naming** — a computed *pattern of non-presence*: participations without appearances at the level of the material. Doctrine already governs it — a silence may be a **cue** and never climbs the gradient; a human may ground it, the AI may not.

   The append-only record of such a cue plus the researcher's uptake (ack / dismiss / edit-to-memo) is the formative trace the accompanying layer exists for, and the only place the platform legitimately learns (`design-begleiten.md` V). *Kill:* if surfaced deviations are dismissed without uptake across one full document, the computation is producing noise, not irritation.

   **1d. What was actually wired: passage relatedness with stated shared terms** (`src/lib/server/corpus-structure/relatedness.ts`, built 2026-08-04). Of the three computable deviations in 1c, none was built; what was built instead is the operation underneath all of them and underneath the Anwalt's evidentiary step at once — *given this text, which passages elsewhere in the project share distinctive vocabulary with it, and which words are they*. One function, three entry points (free text / a sequence / a character range), the corpus's TF-IDF space over df-bounded terms from 1b, adjacent sequences excluded because adjacency is the default rather than a finding.
   **Why this one and not the deviations.** It is the part nothing in the tool can do today. `semantic_search` returns a bare number, and on thematically coherent material that number does not discriminate (Δ = 0.003 across four interviews, 1a); `search_documents` answers at document level with no ranking. Here every hit carries its ground — the shared rare terms — which is the property the whole tool is organised around and the one an embedding cosine structurally cannot supply.
   **Three consumers, one function.** (i) `POST /api/projects/[id]/related` — the search over material the researcher does not have; POST rather than GET so search terms over research data never enter a URL. (ii) `search_related_passages` in `ai/base/search-tools.ts` — available to every persona that runs through `runtime/agent.ts` (Coach, Cowork, Critfriend); **the CName tribunal does not run through that path, so the Abduktor cannot reach it** — comparanda before the hypothesis would turn abduction into subsumption. (iii) the **Anwalt's escalation (C-2 only)**: when C-1 itself declares that i-1/i+1 do not ground the hypothesis and emits `[NEED-MEMO-SEARCH: …]`, those declared terms now query the *material* as well as the memos. The query is the advocate's own articulated need, not a similarity to its own sequence — which is exactly the difference between a test and the neighbourhood that failed in 1a. **D (Richter) is deliberately left alone**, as in 1 and 1a; the passage hits are recorded per sequence as `C-2-passageHits` in `.tmp-forensic-trace/{runId}.jsonl`. *Kill:* if the advocacy prose does not cite the passages it was given, or the ISOMORPH rate rises without the labels getting sharper, it comes out of C-2 — the search and the persona tool stand on their own.
   *Superseded by this:* the unmerged comparanda spike (2026-06-13), which wired embedding comparanda into C-1 and C-2 behind `TQDA_CNAME_COMPARANDA`. Its retrieval is the one 1a falsified; its **prompt discipline** is the part worth keeping and was carried over — locate the hypothesis, never defer to the passages; name the discriminating dimension; a passage that shares words but makes a different move is a lead, not a verdict.
2. **Rejection as a positive trace (researcher side).** Make researcher-rejection a first-class logged act (a downward designation, or withdraw-with-reason, `by = researcher`), so "what the collective rejected, and why" is analyzable — consistent with the silence-as-positive-finding doctrine. *Kill:* if rejections are never revisited analytically, drop.
3. **FI/RI as a separate, document-agnostic pass.** Revisit only *if* (1) shows reconstruction still starves. Prototype one formulating/reflecting reconstruction on one document; test whether its *reflecting* move (Orientierungsrahmen) yields cues distinct from SName open-coding and CName abduction. *Kill:* no analytic distinctness → drop.
4. **A collective-orientation object.** Explore whether a phase / perspective-naming can carry a *shared* orientation (across researchers or cases) without collapsing to a single `by`. *Kill:* if indistinguishable from an ordinary phase, drop.
